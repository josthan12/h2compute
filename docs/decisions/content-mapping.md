Only Python programming, algorithms/data structures and relational databases/SQL belong to this MVP. Broader or compound outcomes are marked partial when only their selected portions are taught. Proposed mappings are planned coverage, not published coverage. NoSQL and other omitted areas remain visible gaps without entering the MVP.

Validate runnable examples on the pinned runtime before publishing. Content must be original and reviewed. Revisit on a syllabus update or when assessed scope changes.
